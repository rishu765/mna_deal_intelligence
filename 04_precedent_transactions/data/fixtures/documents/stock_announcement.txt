[Section: Merger consideration]
BridgeMint shareholders will receive 0.45 Meridian Cloud shares and USD 2.00 in cash for each BridgeMint share. The announcement described an aggregate transaction value of approximately USD 510 million.

[Section: Completion]
The merger completed on 15 September 2023 after shareholder and regulatory approvals.

[Section: Target financial information]
BridgeMint reported FY2022 revenue of USD 80 million, reported EBITDA of USD 9 million, adjusted EBITDA of USD 12 million, EBIT of USD 6 million, and net income of USD 3 million.
