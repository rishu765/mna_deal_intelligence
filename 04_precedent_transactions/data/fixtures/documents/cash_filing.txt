[Section: Offer terms]
The filing states that each Ledgerlane share will receive USD 10.50 in cash. The disclosed equity purchase price is USD 420 million. At 31 December 2023 Ledgerlane had funded debt of USD 12 million and cash of USD 7 million.

[Section: Target financial information]
Ledgerlane reported revenue of USD 96 million and adjusted EBITDA of USD 18 million for the twelve months ended 31 December 2023.
