[Section: Offer terms]
The filing states that each Ledgerlane share will receive USD 10.50 in cash. The disclosed equity purchase price is USD 420 million. Northstar expects to assume no funded debt as part of the transaction.

[Section: Target financial information]
Ledgerlane reported revenue of USD 96 million and adjusted EBITDA of USD 18 million for the twelve months ended 31 December 2023.
