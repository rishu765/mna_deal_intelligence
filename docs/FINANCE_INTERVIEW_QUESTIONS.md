# Finance Interview Questions

| Question | Concise answer pointer |
| --- | --- |
| Enterprise value versus equity value? | EV values operations for all capital providers; equity value belongs to shareholders. Bridge with debt, cash, and defined adjustments. |
| Why use trading comps? | Observe how similar public businesses trade; normalize metrics and apply peer multiple statistics to the target. |
| Why precedents often exceed trading comps? | Control premium and expected synergies, subject to market/date/deal differences. |
| What makes a good comparable? | Similar business model, sector, geography, scale, growth, margins, risk, and financial period; document exclusions. |
| LTM versus NTM? | LTM is historical last twelve months; NTM is forecast next twelve months. Numerator and denominator dates must align. |
| EV/revenue versus EV/EBITDA? | Revenue helps when profitability is weak; EBITDA reflects operating profitability but is sensitive to adjustments. |
| What is QoE? | Assessment of sustainable earnings, cash conversion, accounting quality, and nonrecurring or unsupported items. |
| What is an EBITDA add-back? | A supported normalization for a nonrecurring expense; test recurrence, amount, evidence, and buyer acceptance. |
| Why can add-backs be dangerous? | Recurring costs may be mislabeled, adjustments double-counted, or run-rate savings treated as realized. |
| What is working capital? | Operating current assets minus operating current liabilities under deal-defined classifications. |
| What is an NWC peg? | Normalized working-capital level expected to be delivered at close, often based on historical seasonality/trends. |
| What is net debt? | Debt and debt-like items less eligible cash/cash-like items, under transaction definitions. |
| Examples of debt-like items? | Accrued obligations, deferred consideration, unpaid bonuses/taxes, leases or provisions depending on the SPA. |
| How is restricted cash treated? | Usually not freely available to offset debt; classification depends on access and transaction terms. |
| Why customer concentration matters? | Revenue loss or bargaining power can impair forecast earnings, valuation, covenants, and deal protections. |
| How does diligence affect valuation? | Adjusted EBITDA changes multiple-derived EV; net debt/NWC change equity proceeds; risks affect range and protections. Avoid double counting. |
| Why not blend comps and precedents automatically? | Methods have distinct contexts and assumptions; compare ranges and let judgment determine weighting. |
| What would you study more deeply? | Calendarization, sector metrics, purchase agreements, QoE practice, peg negotiation, debt-like classification, and real peer selection. |
